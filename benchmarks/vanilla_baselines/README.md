## 2. The Non-Trivial Vanilla Baseline Directive

A standard flaw in AI benchmarking is comparing a novel framework against a naive strawman prompt (e.g., *"Summarize this text"*). To prevent this critique, both models in Vanilla mode were prompted with the following rigorous, graduate-level directive:

> *"Please perform an in-depth, rigorous, and critical academic analysis of the following text. Evaluate the validity of the core thesis, the quality and hierarchy of the empirical evidence, the asserted causal mechanisms, and the logical validity of the arguments. Identify any underlying fallacies, unaddressed confounders, or methodological shortcomings."*

Despite being explicitly instructed to evaluate causal mechanisms, fallacies, evidence hierarchies, and confounders, **unconstrained models systematically suffered from cognitive smoothing, skipped quantitative denominators, omitted formal counter-evidence (e.g., Cipriani Lancet meta-analysis), and exhibited zero self-critical auto-falsification.**
