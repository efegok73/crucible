## 2. STEELMANNING

### 2.1 Strongest Formulation of Snowden's Thesis
In *Blacks in Antiquity*, Snowden mounts an empirical refutation of anachronistic claims that post-15th-century biological racism or statutory segregation characterized Greco-Roman civilization. Anchoring his study across `1,300` years from Mycenaean/Homeric horizons to Justinian (`527-565 A.D.` `[P:0008]`, `[P:0039]`, `[P:0164]`), he synthesizes Greek and Latin literary sources, papyri, epigraphy, coins, vase painting, terracottas, bronzes, and `120` plates/figures (`[P:0013-0022]`) to establish four structural propositions:

1. **Methodological Anchoring Before Attribution:** Rather than treating every dark adjective as an Ethiopian marker, Snowden formalizes `6` textual criteria (`[P:0038]`), constructs Table A comparing `8` somatic features in the *Moretum* (`Scybale`) with anthropological descriptions by E. A. Hooton and J. H. Lewis (`[P:0033-0034]`, `[P:0288]`), and requires corroborating physical cues (`>=2` traits `[P:0334]` n.89) in visual art.
2. **Environmental Rather Than Biological Explanation:** Classical ethnographers explained dark skin through climate and solar exposure (`[P:0200]`, `[P:0344]` n.55) rather than innate hereditary inferiority, constructing `"no special racial theory about the inferiority of darker peoples qua darker peoples"` (`[P:0200]`).
3. **Absence of Statutory Caste Bars & Multi-Role Visibility:** Greco-Roman institutions enforced no anti-miscegenation laws or `"sociological Negroes"` descent rules (`[P:0036]`, `[P:0219]`). Africans appeared not solely as enslaved captives (`[P:0210]`) or exotic performers (`[P:0187]`, `[P:0190-0191]`), but as ambassadors, soldiers, athletes, worshippers of Isis, and pupils such as Herodes Atticus' `Memnon` (`[P:0157]`, `[P:0212]`, `[P:0216]`, `[P:0241]`).
4. **Universal Christian Soteriology:** Patristic writers from Origen to Augustine treated Ethiopians as symbolic proof that salvation embraced all humanity regardless of geography or complexion (`[P:0224]`, `[P:0229]`, `[P:0241]`).

### 2.2 Devil's Advocate Filter (Authorial Caveats & Self-Limitations Credited)
A fair forensic reading must credit Snowden's explicit methodological safety valves rather than inventing oversights he already conceded:
- **Nomenclature & Genealogical Caution (`[P:0036]`):** Snowden explicitly states: `"Conclusions as to racial identity or provenance based on nomenclature, however, must be reached with the greatest caution."` He stresses that names such as *Niger*, *Fuscus*, and *Melas* did not establish African extraction and rejects modern one-drop categorization (`[P:0036]`).
- **Demographic Uncertainty (`[P:0144]`, `[P:0207]`, `[P:0210]`):** He repeatedly states that `"it is impossible to estimate the Negro element in the classical world in terms of precise statistics"` (`[P:0207]`) and explicitly discloses that `"A large, doubtlessly the largest, portion of the Ethiopians in Greece and Italy arrived as prisoners of war or as slaves."` (`[P:0210]`)
- **Iconographic Modesty (`[P:0052]`, `[P:0212]`):** On skeletal/artistic subgrouping he writes: `"As to those whom I have classified in the first two groups, not all will agree."` (`[P:0052]`) On elite portraiture he notes: `"we can identify with some degree of certainty only one of the Negroes preserved in marble or bronze"` (`[P:0212]`).
- **Adverse Evidence Openly Logged:** Snowden does not suppress hostile tropes. He documents ominous-color superstitions (Cassius/Brutus, Septimius Severus `[P:0203-0204]`), *Physiognomonica* cowardice associations (`[P:0203]`, `[P:0345]` n.78), Kabeiric caricatures (`[P:0185]`), Juvenal's abortion satire (`[P:0218]`) and `2.23` contrast (`[P:0346]` n.82), Petronius' `taeterrimo` lip description (`[P:0345]` n.76), Pliny's `hi pudore` textual variant (`[P:0344]` n.66), and patristic black=evil imagery citing F. J. Dolger (`[P:0287]` n.34, `[P:0356]` n.19).

### 2.3 Explicit Falsification Criteria

Conversely, his *broad universalist corollary*—that `"antipathy because of color did not arise"` (`[P:0240]`), color was `"ignored"` aesthetically and morally (`[P:0346]` n.82), and early Christian `"Color was inconsequential"` (`[P:0229]`)—is **falsified internally** whenever Snowden's own evidence exposes asymmetrical hermeneutic dismissal, circular text criticism, survivorship skew, or unresolved symbolic condemnation of blackness.

## REAL METHODOLOGICAL SURGERY

### Fracture 1: Asymmetric Evidentiary Thresholds & Retrospective Typological Essentialism (K3 / K5)
Even while rejecting modern `"sociological Negroes"` rules (`[P:0036]`), Snowden constructs his analytical taxonomy upon mid-20th-century essentialist racial biology, treating physical traits as belonging to `"a subtype that anthropologists have designated as the"` `"true” or “purest"` `"type of African Negro."` (`[P:0032]`) alongside `"Nilotic"` and `"black-white cross"` categories (`[P:0032]`, `[P:0048]`). Rather than keeping a strict diagnostic floor when identifying classical figures, Snowden deploys an elastic linguistic escape clause in Chapter I: `"adjectives denoting black, even in the absence of other evidence, must have indicated Ethiopian extraction."` (`[P:0029]`)

This dual standard produces **Consistency Drift** in biographical attributions. Where Snowden's own rule requires corroboration (`[P:0038]`), speculative figures are still enlisted to populate high-status black assimilation: vase-painter *Amasis* (`"He may have been Negroid, as I have argued above."` `[P:0128]`), *Delphos* (`"the allusion may indicate that he was not merely swart or dusky-skinned as Boardman suggests but Negroid."` `[P:0041]`), *Terence* (`"the evidence for the presence of Negroes in Carthage, therefore, point to the possibility that Terence was of Ethiopian or Negroid extraction."` `[P:0294]` n.3), and *Heliodorus* (`"Heliodorus, the author of the Aethiopica, it has been suggested, was a colored man."` `[P:0212]`). Yet immediately adjacent to these suggestions, Snowden grants that `"we can identify with some degree of certainty only one of the Negroes preserved in marble or bronze"` (`[P:0212]`). Tentative `"may"` hypotheses are treated individually as cautious suggestions while functioning cumulatively in his Synthesis as proof of widespread aristocratic renown.

- **Concrete Example (The Elastic Caliper):** Imagine a referee inspecting claims of discrimination with a steel micrometer—refusing to count bias unless a statute articulates explicit biological inferiority—yet inspecting claims of upward social mobility with an elastic rubber tape, counting `"it has been suggested"` biographies and uncertain vase nicknames alongside one confirmed portrait (`Memnon`).

### Fracture 2: Motte-and-Bailey Slippage ("No Legal Caste Bar" -> "No Color Antipathy") & Asymmetric Dismissal of Adverse Testimony (K3 / K5)
Snowden's master narrative slides continuously between a defensible **Motte** (classical law enacted no racial caste bars or miscegenation bans `[P:0200]`, `[P:0219]`) and an overextended **Bailey** (social, aesthetic, and moral prejudice over skin color essentially did not exist): `"antipathy because of color did not arise."` (`[P:0240]`) and `"the total evidence demonstrates that as a whole the Greeks and Romans ignored color in evaluating men aesthetically, morally, or intellectually"` (`[P:0346]` n.82).

To defend that Bailey against hostile primary evidence logged in his own footnotes, Snowden deploys four **Hermeneutic Neutralization Mechanisms**:
1. **Age-Restricted Dismissal:** When documenting adult Roman dread of dark skin—such as Emperor Septimius Severus furiously ordering an Ethiopian soldier removed as an ill omen (`[P:0203-0204]`, `[P:0346]` n.80)—Snowden subordinates imperial testimony to a single ethnographic aphorism from Agatharchides asserting that `"such a fear ceased at childhood"` (`[P:0204]`).
2. **Text-Critical Circularity:** Evaluating whether Pliny (`Naturalis historia` 6.35.190) wrote `hi pudore` (meaning Ethiopians painted themselves red out of shame at black skin), Snowden rejects that manuscript reading by citing `"the usual Greco-Roman attitude toward the Ethiopian’s color, in my judgment, do not support a suggestion which has been made for a difficult line in Elder Pliny"` (`[P:0344]` n.66)—presuming the conclusion to settle a philological variant.
3. **Endnote Quarantine & Proverbial Scrubbing:** Petronius' explicit somatic revulsion (`"seems to imply an"` undesirable `"lip-thickness in the Ethiopian described."` `[P:0345]` n.76) is tucked into an endnote while the main text declares `"the number of expressed preferences for blackness and whiteness in classical literature is approximately equal."` (`[P:0203]`). Similarly, Juvenal `2.23` (`"Aethiopem albus"`—inviting mockery of a black Ethiopian alongside a crippled man) is neutralized as bias-free proverb (`"are simply an illustration of the proverbial ater (Aethiops)-albus"` with `"no overtones of racial bias."` `[P:0346]` n.82).
4. **Denominator Blindness & Diachronic Confounding:** Snowden acknowledges `"A large, doubtlessly the largest, portion of the Ethiopians in Greece and Italy arrived as prisoners of war or as slaves."` (`[P:0210]`), and records spectacles where `100` Ethiopian huntsmen appeared in the circus (`61 B.C.` `[P:0190]`, `[P:0208]`), `300` pairs of gladiators including defeated Blemmyes fought under Probus (`276-282 A.D.` `[P:0161]`, `[P:0191]`), and Nero spent `300,000` sesterces freeing the actor Glycon (`[P:0187]`). Yet marble busts—which depict only `"Negroes of the less humble social classes"` (`[P:0212]`)—and poetic verses (`[P:0203]`) cannot measure everyday treatment of the unlettered captive majority. Moreover, topical synthesis across `1,300` years (`[P:0008]`) acts as a **diachronic confounder**, leveling idealized Homeric myth (`[P:0168]`) with violent Roman border wars against Nubians (`24-22 B.C.` `[P:0156]`) and Blemmyes (`[P:0161]`).

- **Concrete Example (The Courtroom Docket Fallacy):** Concluding that medieval peasants faced no social disdain because royal charters contained no anti-peasant statute and poets occasionally praised rustic simplicity confuses *absence of statutory prohibition* with *absence of hierarchical stigma*. Likewise, counting elite busts (`[P:0212]`) while missing the daily experience of the war-captive `"largest, portion"` (`[P:0210]`) measures the visible tip and ignores the submerged denominator.

### Fracture 3: The Patristic Chromatic Paradox — Conflating Soteriological Universalism with Somatic Neutrality (K3 / K5)
Chapter IX documents early Christian theological rejection of geographical exclusion (`[P:0220-0239]`), yet commits a severe **Equivocation** between *moral universality of redemption* and *symbolic neutrality of black skin*. Snowden summarizes the Fathers by claiming `"Color was inconsequential; in fact, we have seen that they regarded as black all men who had not been illumined by God’s light and considered all men, regardless of the color of the skin, as potentially Christians."` (`[P:0229]`)

His own quotations invert that neutrality into a strict moral hierarchy where blackness symbolizes spiritual degradation and salvation requires chromatic erasure:
- **Sin Coded as Blackness:** Origen writes of sinners who `"because darkened with many serious sins and stained with the dye of wickedness, have become black and dark."` (`[P:0227]`), and Jerome interprets Abdimelech as `"a foreigner of dark and ignoble race"` representing the Gentiles (`[P:0227]`).
- **Salvation Coded as Whitening:** Augustine states `"Christ came into the world to make blacks radiantly bright"` (`[P:0224]`), Paulinus admonishes `"They are indeed called black but let them not remain black"` (`[P:0228]`), and Gregory of Nyssa affirms `"Christ came into the world to make blacks white and that in the Kingdom of Heaven Ethiopians become white."` (`[P:0229]`). Interpreting a spiritual humility ordeal constructed precisely out of an ethnic insult (`"You are not a man"`) as somatic endorsement conflates *spiritual equality in baptism* with *symbolic equality of color*.

- **Concrete Example (Redemption Through Chromatic Erasure):** Saying "you are completely welcome inside our academy, provided all traces of your rust become gleaming silver upon graduation" affirms equal eligibility for admission while explicitly coding rust as corruption. Christian soteriology granted spiritual inclusion while preserving—and moralizing—an asymmetrical dark-evil / white-virtue chromatic code.

## FALLACY AUDIT

1. **Motte-and-Bailey Fallacy (`[P:0200]`, `[P:0219]` vs. `[P:0240]`, `[P:0346]`):** Shifts seamlessly from the verified claim that classical antiquity `"developed no special racial theory about the inferiority of darker peoples qua darker peoples"` (`[P:0200]`) and `"No laws in the Greco-Roman world prohibited unions of blacks and whites."` (`[P:0219]`) to the sweeping assertion that `"antipathy because of color did not arise."` (`[P:0240]`). *Example:* Inferring from the absence of statutory marriage bans that social mockery or ominous prejudice never occurred.
2. **Text-Critical Circularity / *Petitio Principii* (`[P:0344]` n.66):** Rejects Mayhoff's `hi pudore` reading in Pliny (`Naturalis historia` 6.35.190) in part because `"the usual Greco-Roman attitude toward the Ethiopian’s color, in my judgment, do not support a suggestion which has been made for a difficult line in Elder Pliny"` (`[P:0344]`). *Example:* Using a contested hypothesis ("Greeks/Romans held no color shame view") as the referee to disqualify textual counter-evidence (`hi pudore`).
3. **Survivorship & Denominator Fallacy (`[P:0207]`, `[P:0210]`, `[P:0212]`):** Concedes both statistical impossibility (`[P:0207]`) and that war captives/slaves comprised `"doubtlessly the largest, portion"` (`[P:0210]`), yet treats surviving bronze/marble elite busts (`[P:0212]`) and balanced literary epigrams (`[P:0203]`) as an unbiased cross-section of Greco-Roman social experience. *Example:* Judging life across a plantation estate solely by portraits commissioned for its freed estate managers.
4. **Soteriological-Somatic Equivocation (`[P:0224]`, `[P:0227-0229]`, `[P:0234-0235]`):** Equates Christian openness to baptizing Ethiopians with `"Color was inconsequential"` (`[P:0229]`), despite quoting `"in the Kingdom of Heaven Ethiopians become white."` (`[P:0229]`) and `"you are not a man"` (`[P:0234]`). *Example:* Confusing "anyone may join the guild regardless of dialect" with "the guild treats every dialect as equally prestigious" when initiation requires losing the dialect.
5. **Modal Escalation & Speculative Stacking (`[P:0029]`, `[P:0041]`, `[P:0128]`, `[P:0212]`, `[P:0294]` n.3):** Upgrades hedged inferences (`"may indicate"` `[P:0041]`, `"He may have been Negroid"` `[P:0128]`, `"it has been suggested"` `[P:0212]`, `"point to the possibility"` `[P:0294]`) into collective evidence of prestigious integration despite admitting only one portrait can be identified securely (`[P:0212]`). *Example:* Stacking four uncertain suspects into one conviction on the ground that all four *might* fit the description.
